The usual questions.

Will you get up to speed fast enough?

Faster than a new hire. I've been through enough of these to know where to start and what to ignore.

How is this different from an agency?

An agency sends you deliverables. I work inside your team and own the outcome, at whatever level you need. Some weeks that's advising you and your leads on the hard calls; other weeks it's hands on the work myself. One senior person accountable to you, not a brief handed to a pod.

What happens when you leave?

I work myself out of the job. By the end, your team owns the story and the system, and I'm a phone call, not a dependency.

Can you go deep on technical products?

Yes. I can get deep enough on the product to do the marketing myself, and I know how to work with the SMEs and technical teams in your org, so the story reaches the right people in the way they actually evaluate.
